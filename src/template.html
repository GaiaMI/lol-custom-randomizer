How it works

lol-custom-randomizer is a free League of Legends tool for custom games with friends. It splits ten players into balanced teams, deals out roles, draws champions one by one, and can add random builds and runes. It also works for a five-player Flex stack. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

FAQ

Do I have to enter ranks?

No, ranks are optional. Without any rank, teams are shuffled completely at random. With ranks, the site keeps the fairest splits. You can also fill in only some players: the others count as the average of the known ranks.

Do I have to enter ten names?

No. Empty names stay as Player 1, Player 2 and so on, and every draw still works.

How are teams balanced?

Each division adds about 10% to a player's weight, so high ranks count for much more. The site compares the 252 possible 5v5 splits, keeps the closest ones and picks one at random.

Can I keep two players on different teams?

Yes. Add the pair in "Players to put on different teams", and every team shuffle will respect it.

Can I force a role or a champion on a player?

Yes. "Player role" keeps someone in a role on every shuffle. Champion role, class, damage, range and difficulty limit the champions they can get. You can tick several options, and discreet mode hides it all from their line.

How does Flex 5 work?

Flex 5 is for a stack of five with no opposing team. Each player gets a role and a champion. Switching between 5v5 and Flex 5 clears the current draw.

What are counters and preys?

A counter is a champion that beats the one facing it. A prey is a champion it beats. The numbers are LoLalytics matchups in Emerald and above, between players of the same role.

Can several people use the link at the same time?

Yes. Everything runs in each person's browser, so nobody affects anyone else. It also means each person has their own draw: one person draws, then shares their screen or uses "Copy teams".

Are my settings saved?

Yes, in your browser only. They are never sent to a server. Settings are lost in private browsing and don't follow you to another device. The current draw is not saved.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account and nothing to install. This is a fan tool, not affiliated with Riot Games.
